# DCRG Phase 5: The Zero-Override Contradiction Veto Filter

> **Specification Reference:** DCRG-ARCH-005 Section 4  
> **Status:** Operational & Interlocked  

---

## 1. The Zero-Override Principle

> **FUNDAMENTAL SAFETY INVARIANT:** A high composite score generated by one modality **CANNOT** override a direct directional contradiction asserted by another modality.

Even if an operator speaks `"FORWARD"` with $100\%$ confidence and environmental context is completely clear, if the operator's physical hand is pulling the joystick `"BACKWARD"`, this discrepancy represents an active hazard or operator confusion. Under the Zero-Override Law, the Fusion Engine immediately asserts a contradiction veto and caps the Command Reliability Score at a maximum ceiling of $30.0$.

---

## 2. Geometric Contradiction Predicate

A contradiction is asserted when either of the following two predicates evaluates to `TRUE`:

### Predicate 1: Directional Contradiction
$$\text{Contradiction}_{\text{dir}}(\vec{V}_{\text{voice}}, \vec{J}_{\text{norm}}) \iff \left( \cos(\Delta \theta) \le 0.0 \land C_{\text{whisper}} \ge 0.70 \land I_{\text{joy}} \ge 0.30 \right)$$

- **$\cos(\Delta \theta) \le 0.0$:** The angular separation $\Delta \theta$ between the spoken command and physical joystick deflection is $90^\circ$ or greater (perpendicular or directly opposing).
- **$C_{\text{whisper}} \ge 0.70$:** The speech recognition confidence meets or exceeds the contradiction threshold (prevents low-confidence or garbled speech from triggering false vetos).
- **$I_{\text{joy}} \ge 0.30$:** The physical deflection intensity meets or exceeds the contradiction threshold (prevents minor accidental hand tremors from triggering vetos).

### Predicate 2: Spoken `STOP` vs Active Joystick
$$\text{Contradiction}_{\text{stop}} \iff \left( \text{Command} = \text{"STOP"} \land C_{\text{whisper}} \ge 0.70 \land I_{\text{joy}} \ge 0.30 \right)$$

- The operator commands `"STOP"` while physically pushing or deflecting the joystick.

---

## 3. Veto Interlock Enforcement

When a contradiction is detected:
1. **Veto Flag:** `contradiction_detected = True` and `veto_asserted = True`.
2. **Ceiling Clamping:** $\text{CRS} \leftarrow \min(\text{CRS}_{\text{raw}}, 30.0)$.
3. **Analytical Finding:** `analytical_finding = AnalyticalFinding.CONTRADICTION`.
4. **Transparent Reason:** A descriptive string is published detailing the exact tokens, directions, intensities, and cosine similarity.
5. **Phase 6 Safety Interlock:** When Phase 6 consumes this bundle, the veto flag forces the state machine into `RED` (Command Hold / Refusal), bypassing `GREEN`.
